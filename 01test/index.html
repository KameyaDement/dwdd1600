Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using the internet, technology, and digital devices safely, responsibly, respectfully, and ethically.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explain how people are allowed to use computers, the internet, and other technology in a school, workplace, or organization.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, or work and claim them as your own without giving credit.

Simple definition: Plagiarism means taking someone else's work or ideas and pretending they are yours.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty means cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork to gain an unfair advantage.

Simple definition: Academic dishonesty is breaking school rules to unfairly improve your grades or academic results.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

Simple definition: AI-generated content should be used honestly, responsibly, and according to your school's rules.
