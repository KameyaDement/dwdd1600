Who Were the Signers

Famouse Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the Declaration of Independence were:

Thomas Jefferson
Virginia delegate and primary author of the Declaration of Independence. He helped express the colonies' desire for freedom from Great Britain.
John Adams
Massachusetts delegate who strongly supported independence and helped persuade other delegates to support the cause. He also served on the committee that drafted the Declaration.
Benjamin Franklin
Pennsylvania delegate who was a famous scientist, inventor, diplomat, and statesman. He helped edit and support the Declaration of Independence.
John Hancock
Massachusetts delegate and president of the Continental Congress. He is famous for his large and distinctive signature on the Declaration.
Samuel Adams
Massachusetts delegate and influential revolutionary who strongly advocated for colonial independence from Great Britain.
George Walton
Georgia delegate who signed the Declaration of Independence and later served as governor of Georgia.
John Witherspoon
New Jersey delegate, minister, and president of the College of New Jersey, now known as Princeton University. He was the only active college president to sign the Declaration.

The signers of the Declaration of Independence played an important role in the founding of the United States. Leaders such as Thomas Jefferson, John Adams, Benjamin Franklin, and John Hancock helped express the colonies' desire for freedom and independence from Great Britain. Their decision to sign the Declaration showed their commitment to creating a new nation based on liberty, equality, and individual rights.
